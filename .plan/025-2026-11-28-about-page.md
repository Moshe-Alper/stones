# 025 — About Page (אודות)

Status: done
Owner: Moshik Alper
Last updated: 2026-11-28 (live via PR #13, a891cd2)

## Goal
Add an "about" page (`/[locale]/about`, Hebrew title **"אודות"**) that tells the Alper family story and shows the factory and the craft. It should build trust with families who are choosing who will make the memorial stone. The page is short, personal and photo-led, like the rest of the site, and it ends with a clear contact CTA.

## Background
- The site has never had an about page. No `about` route appears anywhere in the git history.
- Plan 022 (2026-11-25) kept new pages, about included, out of scope **on purpose**. This plan reopens that decision for the about page only, at the owner's request (2026-11-28).
- Material we already have that fits an about page:
  - "עסק משפחתי מאז 1965" (`FamilyNote`), the hero line "מצבות משיש ואבן, מאז 1965"
  - Footer blurb: the factory and offices are in Pardes Hana, next to the cemetery
  - `OurFactory` component and `factory-photos.ts`. They are hidden on the homepage until there are enough photos (plan 023), so the about page is a natural home for them.
  - `ProcessSteps` (consultation → design → production at the factory → installation)
- New photo (2026-11-28): a craftsman's hand next to a pneumatic grinder with a diamond bit, carving a relief into light limestone. It is saved as `frontend/public/images/factory/carving-detail.webp` and is not used yet.
  - 574×1020, portrait, about 100 KB. That is fine for a side image or a tile, but **too small for a full-width hero**.
  - It has no inscriptions or names, so it is fine for a bereavement context.
  - It fits the existing `tools` slot ("כלי עבודה וחריטה") in `factory-photos.ts`.

## Scope
In scope (`frontend/`, all 3 locales):
1. New route `frontend/src/app/[locale]/about/page.tsx` + `page.module.css`, built from `Header`, `Breadcrumb`, `Footer` and `ContactBand`, following the same structure as `accessibility/page.tsx`.
2. Page sections, in order (see Q2):
   1. **Opening**: H1 "אודות שיש אלפר" and one or two lines: a family business since 1965, a factory in Pardes Hana.
   2. **Our story**: 2–3 short paragraphs on the founding, the generations, and how the work is done today. Facts come from the owner (Q3).
   3. **The craft**: the new carving photo next to a short text about handwork, carving and lettering.
   4. **The factory**: reuse `OurFactory` (the photo strip with placeholders) or a smaller version. See Q4.
   5. **How we work**: reuse `ProcessSteps` as is.
   6. `ContactBand`, the closing CTA.
3. Messages: a new `AboutPage` namespace in `messages/{he,en,ru}.json` (`metaTitle`, `title`, `intro`, `storyHeading`, `storyParagraphs[]`, `craftHeading`, `craftText`, `craftImageAlt`).
4. Navigation: add "אודות" to the header nav (`Header.navAbout`) and to the footer quick links (`Footer.navAbout`). See Q5.
5. Image: set `{ id: "tools", file: "carving-detail.webp" }` in `factory-photos.ts`, and use the same photo in the craft section.
6. SEO: `generateMetadata` with a title and description; add the route to the sitemap if one exists.
7. Tests:
   - a unit test for the page (renders the H1 and the story paragraphs)
   - Header/Footer tests updated for the new link
   - an axe e2e run on `/about` in he/en/ru (added to the plan 024 spec)
8. Docs: `.doc/architecture.md` (new route) and the plan 022 note that this one exception was approved.

Out of scope:
- Other new pages from plan 022 (guides/blog, testimonials, press, monuments)
- Team bios with personal photos of family members (unless the owner wants them, see Q3)
- Bringing `OurFactory` back to the homepage
- Video

## Assumptions
- Hebrew text is written fresh by Claude from the owner's facts, and the owner approves it before it goes live. EN/RU are Claude's drafts and are reviewed later, as in earlier plans.
- No claims we cannot back up (no "the biggest", "the best", invented numbers or invented awards).
- The tone is the same as the rest of the site: calm, personal, not salesy. Use "מפעל", not "סטודיו" (plan 022).
- The photo has no GPS or EXIF data to strip (it is a webp re-exported by chat). It will be checked again before commit.
