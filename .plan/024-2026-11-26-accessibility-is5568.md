# 024 — Website Accessibility (IS 5568 / WCAG AA) and Accessibility Statement

Status: done
Owner: Moshik Alper

## Goal
Bring the site to the level Israeli law requires, IS 5568 (based on WCAG 2.0) at **level AA**, and publish a real accessibility statement page. **AAA is not required by law and is not a goal.** No accessibility badge or claim goes back on the site until the work is done and checked (see plan 023).

## Background (what the law says, checked 2026-11-26)
- Regulation 35א of the accessibility regulations (התאמות נגישות לשירות) requires websites that serve the public to meet IS 5568 **at level AA**.
- Exemptions, per Kol Zchut (page updated 26.08.2026). Check them with an accountant or lawyer, because these are not legal advice:
  - Exempt dealer (עוסק פטור): fully exempt.
  - Average annual turnover under 120,000 ₪: exempt for 3 years, renewable.
  - 120,000–1,000,000 ₪: the exemption covers only sites that existed before 26.10.2017. **This site is new (2026), so it would have to be accessible.** The same page also mentions an "automatic" exemption up to 1,000,000 ₪, so the wording conflicts.
  - Above 1,000,000 ₪: no automatic exemption.
- Enforcement: lawsuits without proof of harm, up to about 50,000 ₪. Sites are commonly targeted by serial plaintiffs. That is why it is worth doing even if an exemption might apply.

## Scope
In scope (`frontend/`, all 3 locales):
1. **Automated audit**: add `@axe-core/playwright` and an e2e spec that runs axe (tags `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`) on home, catalog, a category page and contact, in he/en/ru.
2. **Manual audit** of every page:
   - keyboard-only navigation
   - visible focus on all interactive elements (header nav, language switcher, WhatsApp FAB, FAQ, gallery, pagination, map)
   - screen reader (VoiceOver) pass in Hebrew
   - 200% zoom / 320px reflow
   - color contrast on the stone/gold palette
3. **Fixes** found by 1–2. Expected ones:
   - skip-to-content link and `<main>` landmark
   - meaningful `alt` on photos, empty `alt` on decorative images
   - accessible names on icon-only buttons and links (WhatsApp, phone, map)
   - contact form: labels, error messages tied with `aria-describedby`, `aria-live` for submit status
   - gallery/lightbox focus trap and Esc
   - `prefers-reduced-motion`
   - `lang` on mixed-language text
4. **Accessibility statement page** `/[locale]/accessibility` (הצהרת נגישות). It must include:
   - the standard and level (IS 5568, AA)
   - what was checked and when
   - known limitations
   - an accessibility contact person with phone and email
   - the date of the last update

   Link it from the footer in all locales.
5. Docs: update `.doc/architecture.md` (new route) and `.doc/glossary.md` if new terms appear.

Out of scope:
- WCAG AAA
- third-party accessibility overlay widgets ("נגישות" toolbar plugins). They do not make a site compliant and are often criticized.
- a paid certificate from an accessibility consultant (see Q2)

## Implementation Notes (2026-11-26)
Branch `plan/024-accessibility` (from `origin/main`). Not committed yet.

**Audit results**
- axe (wcag2a/2aa/21a/21aa) on home, catalog, a category page and contact in he/en/ru found **0 violations** before any fix, colour contrast included. The site was already in good shape.
- The manual review found these gaps, all now fixed:
  - no skip link
  - no `<main>` focus target
  - no focus ring designed for dark/photo backgrounds (only the browser default)
  - no `prefers-reduced-motion`
  - the lightbox did not move focus in, trap Tab or return focus
  - the form named no specific field on error (no `aria-invalid`)
  - the Hebrew address was not marked `lang="he"` on EN/RU, and `aria-label` overrode its text
- Reflow at 320px: no horizontal scroll on home, category, contact or the statement page.

**Changes**
- `src/app/globals.css`: `:focus-visible` ring, `.skip-link`, `.visually-hidden`, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`: skip link. All pages: `<main id="main-content" tabIndex={-1}>`.
- `CategoryGallery`: focus into the dialog, Tab stays on the close button, Esc closes, focus returns to the thumbnail.
- `ContactForm`: empty required fields get `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` pointing to the status message, and focus moves to the first one.
- `ContactDetails` / `ContactMap`: the address has `lang="he" dir="rtl"`, and the label is now visually hidden text instead of `aria-label`.
- New page `src/app/[locale]/accessibility/` with messages `AccessibilityPage` (he/en/ru), plus the footer link `Footer.navAccessibility`.
- Tests: `e2e/accessibility.spec.ts` (axe on 15 page×locale combinations, skip link, lightbox focus), plus vitest for the form, the gallery and the footer.
